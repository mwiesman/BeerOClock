## App Name

## Subtitle / Short Description

## Description
Ever wondered how many beers a brisket takes? Now you'll know.

Beer O'Clock translates cooking times into "Cold Ones" — your personal beer-drinking time unit. Time yourself drinking a beer, and every recipe tells you exactly how many beers that cook takes.

Features:
- Pour One Out — tilt-to-pour beer animation
- Time a Cold One — stopwatch or manual calibration
- 10 built-in grilling recipes with Cold One conversions
- Step-by-step cook timer with Cold One reminders
- Choose your glass: pint, mug, bottle, or can
- Beautiful skeuomorphic design inspired by classic iOS

Free with no ads. Buy the developer a Cold One if you enjoy it!
