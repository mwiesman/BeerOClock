# Privacy Policy — Beer O'Clock

**Last Updated: March 2026**

Beer O'Clock is a cooking timer app that measures cook times in "Cold Ones" — your personal beer-drinking time unit. We built this app to be fun, useful, and respectful of your privacy.

## The Short Version

Beer O'Clock does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

## What Data Is Stored

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device using AsyncStorage:

- **Cold One time** — how long it takes you to drink a beer (used to convert recipe cook times)
- **Glass style preference** — your chosen glass display style
- **Reminders setting** — whether you want drink reminders during cooking

This data never leaves your device. There are no servers, no accounts, and no cloud sync.

## What We Do NOT Collect

- No personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- No location data
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising identifiers
- No cookies or web tracking
- No device fingerprinting
- No data shared with third parties

## In-App Purchases

Beer O'Clock may offer optional tip purchases to support development. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The app itself does not collect, store, or have access to any payment or billing information.

## Third-Party Services

Beer O'Clock is built with Expo (React Native). Expo is used as a development framework only. In production builds, no data is sent to Expo or any other third-party service.

## Children's Privacy

Because Beer O'Clock collects no data from anyone, there is no data collected from children under 13 (or any other age group). The app does not require account creation or any personal information to use.

Note: Beer O'Clock references alcoholic beverages and is intended for users of legal drinking age.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be reflected here with an updated date. Since the app collects no data, meaningful changes are unlikely.
